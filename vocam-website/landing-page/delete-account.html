Permanent Action

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please review all information carefully before proceeding.

Before You Delete

We're sorry to see you go! Before deleting your account, please consider:

  • Temporary break: You can simply uninstall the app and reinstall later to keep your progress
  • Data export: Consider saving your vocabulary lists manually if you want to keep your progress
  • Feedback: Let us know how we can improve at vocam.app@gmail.com

How to Delete Your Account

Method 1

  1. In-App: Use the account deletion feature in the app settings (on your profile page)

Method 2

  1. Send us a deletion request using the form below or email us directly
  2. Include your account email and reason for deletion (optional)
  3. We'll confirm your identity and process the request within 7 business days
  4. Receive confirmation once your account has been completely deleted
  5. Account deletion is complete - you can create a new account with the same email if desired

Data We Cannot Delete

For legal and security reasons, some data must be retained:

  • Legal compliance records: Account deletion requests and related communications (7 years)
  • Anonymized analytics: Usage statistics with all personal identifiers removed (24 months)
  • Security logs: Login attempts and security events for fraud prevention (12 months)
  • Financial records: Payment information if applicable (retained per legal requirements)

Note: This retained data cannot be used to identify you personally and complies with GDPR and CCPA requirements.

Legal Information

Processing Time: Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days of verification.

Data Portability: Before deletion, you may request a copy of your data by emailing us.

Third-Party Services: Data processed by Google Cloud services (Vision API, Speech-to-Text) is deleted according to their data retention policies.

Compliance: Our deletion process complies with GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and Google Play Store requirements.

Verification Required: We verify account ownership before processing deletion requests to prevent unauthorized deletions.
