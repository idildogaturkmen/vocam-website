Learning Made Visual

Traditional language learning can be abstract and disconnected from real life. Vocam bridges that gap by connecting words with the objects you see every day.

Simply point your camera at any object—a cup, book, chair, or apple—and instantly discover how to say it in your target language. With example sentences, pronunciation guides, and spaced repetition, you'll build vocabulary that sticks.

Perfect for travelers, students, and anyone wanting to learn languages in a more natural, contextual way.
